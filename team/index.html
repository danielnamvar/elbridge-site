About Elbridge

The Elbridge Group (“Elbridge”) is a private real estate banking firm specialized in providing flexible capital across the stack for special situations in the middle and upper middle market.

With decades of collective experience and billions in capital deployed, Elbridge has mastered the ability to swiftly assess transactions and fund with creativity and efficiency.

Financing

Elbridge provides timely, market-smart debt and equity placement, supported by limited discretionary capital to close the gaps the market won’t fill. New deals may be submitted to deals@elbridge.com.

Terms shown for Elbridge’s direct lending and investment programs are indicative and subject to change pending the specific circumstances of a transaction, market conditions, and available liquidity.

Elbridge arranges debt and equity for commercial real estate owners, developers, and operators nationwide. We work across the full capital stack, from senior mortgage debt through joint venture equity, and stay structure-agnostic: the right capital for a transaction is whatever closes it on terms the sponsor can live with.

Most engagements begin with a situation rather than a product. A maturing loan with no obvious takeout. A partner who needs to be bought out. A construction budget that moved. An asset that will not pencil at today’s rates without a piece of subordinate capital. We underwrite the transaction first, then take it to the lenders and investors whose current appetite actually fits it, rather than running every deal past the same short list.

How we invest

Elbridge focuses primarily on short-duration loans secured by improved commercial real estate, generally in first position. On a limited basis, the firm also invests in subordinate debt and alternative equity through managed accounts.

Across these structures, the firm applies a credit-driven approach: underwriting to underlying asset value, emphasizing downside protection, and identifying a credible path to repayment or exit.

Selective by design

Elbridge’s intermediary platform is a primary source of proprietary deal flow, supported by decades of commercial real estate experience and longstanding industry relationships. It gives the firm access to opportunities at attractive entry points without requiring it to commit investment capital to every transaction.

The asset management business invests selectively in that flow, choosing only those opportunities that meet its underwriting standards and preserving the flexibility to wait for the right basis, structure, and terms.

Ways to participate

Pooled funds

Pooled vehicles for accredited investors seeking current income through portfolios of short-duration, first-lien real estate loans, with an emphasis on principal preservation. Elbridge Mortgage Fund I LLC is closed to new investment.

Separately managed accounts

Customized mandates for institutions and family offices, tailored to investment size, geography, asset type, structure, and duration. Select accounts may also invest in subordinate debt and alternative equity transactions with repeat Elbridge sponsors, subject to available liquidity. Investors can retain approval over individual investments.

Institutional lending partnerships

Origination and asset management services for lenders and funds, spanning sourcing, underwriting, closing, servicing, and workout support. Elbridge works with a limited number of partners at a time.
